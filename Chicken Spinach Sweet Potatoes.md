Chicken, sweet potatoes, mushrooms and spinach baked together in a creamy coconut milk gravy.

![Chicken with spinach, sweet potatoes and mushrooms](https://paleoleap.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/chicken-spinach-potatoes-mushrooms-recipe.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                                       | Half batch | As written (4 servings) | Double batch |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------ |
| Chicken breasts, skinless, boneless and sliced   | 1 lb       | 2 lbs                   | 4 lbs        |
| Sweet potatoes, peeled and diced                 | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Mushrooms, sliced                                | 5          | 10                      | 20           |
| Red onion, sliced                                | ½          | 1                       | 2            |
| Garlic cloves, minced                            | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Baby spinach                                     | 1 cup      | 2 cups                  | 4 cups       |
| Chicken stock                                    | ¼ cup      | ½ cup                   | 1 cup        |
| Coconut milk                                     | ½ cup      | 1 cup                   | 2 cups       |
| Garlic powder                                    | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Onion powder                                     | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Paprika                                          | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Coconut oil                                      | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper         | to taste   | to taste                | to taste     |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Combine the garlic powder, onion powder and paprika in a bowl, then season to taste.
3. Season the chicken pieces with the spice mixture.
4. Melt the coconut oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Brown the chicken on both sides, 2 to 3 minutes per side, then transfer it to a baking dish.
6. Cook the diced sweet potatoes in the skillet over medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes, adding more coconut oil if needed.
7. Add the onion, mushrooms and garlic to the sweet potatoes and cook another 2 to 3 minutes.
8. Add the sweet potatoes, onion, mushrooms and garlic to the baking dish.
9. Pour in the coconut milk and chicken stock.
10. Top with the baby spinach and give everything a toss.
11. Bake covered for 18 to 20 minutes, then uncovered for another 10 minutes.
