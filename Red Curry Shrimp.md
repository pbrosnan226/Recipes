Red curry paste fried in coconut cream, then simmered with zucchini, carrot, peas and shrimp, and finished off the heat with cilantro, mint and lime.

## Instructions

1. Open the can of coconut milk and scoop off ¼ cup of the thick cream. Put it in a large 12-inch non-stick skillet over medium-high heat and cook until the liquid starts to bubble furiously. Add the red curry paste, reduce the heat to medium-low, and stir with a wooden spoon until combined. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the curry paste is very fragrant, about three minutes.
2. Add the zucchini and carrots, turn the heat to medium, and stir until the pieces are completely covered in the paste. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables just start to soften, about 3 minutes.
3. Pour in the remaining coconut milk and stir well. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce the heat to a steady simmer. Add the jalapeños now, if you're using them. Cook until the vegetables are completely tender, about 7 minutes. Add the shrimp and peas and cook until the shrimp are pink and plump, about 2 minutes.
4. Stir the fish sauce, chicken stock and sugar together in a medium bowl. Once the sugar has dissolved, pour it into the skillet. Stir well and simmer for a minute. Turn off the heat and add the cilantro, chopped mint and lime juice. Serve with the whole mint leaves as a garnish and a side of white rice.
