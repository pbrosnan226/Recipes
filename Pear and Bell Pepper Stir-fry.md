Ground beef stir-fried with bell peppers, zucchini, mushrooms, carrot and sliced pear, then finished with beef stock reduced by half.

![Pear and bell pepper ground beef stir-fry](https://paleoleap.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/pear-bell-pepper-ground-beef-stir-fry-recipe.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                          | Half batch | As written (4 servings) | Double batch |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------ |
| Ground beef                         | ¾ lb       | 1½ lbs                  | 3 lbs        |
| Bell peppers, sliced                | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Onion, sliced                       | ½          | 1                       | 2            |
| Zucchini, sliced                    | ½          | 1                       | 2            |
| Mushrooms, sliced                   | 4 oz       | 8 oz                    | 16 oz        |
| Pear, sliced                        | ½          | 1                       | 2            |
| Carrots, grated                     | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Garlic cloves, minced               | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Fresh ginger, minced                | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Green onions, sliced, to garnish    | 1          | 2                       | 4            |
| Beef stock                          | ½ cup      | 1 cup                   | 2 cups       |
| Cooking fat                         | as needed  | as needed               | as needed    |

## Instructions

1. Melt some cooking fat in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook until soft and fragrant, about 1 to 2 minutes.
3. Brown the ground beef until no longer pink, 4 to 5 minutes.
4. Drain some of the fat if you like, then add all the remaining ingredients except the beef stock.
5. Cook until the vegetables are nearly soft, with just a hint of crunch left.
6. Pour in the beef stock and cook until reduced by half.
7. Serve topped with the green onions.
