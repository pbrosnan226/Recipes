An adaptable breakfast casserole — sausage, mushrooms, bell peppers, spinach and cheddar over cubes of crusty bread. It can be assembled and refrigerated up to 24 hours before baking.

![Breakfast casserole](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/breakfast-casserole-2.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                                  | Half batch | As written (12 servings)        | Double batch |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Cubes of crusty bread                       | 2 cups     | 4 cups (175g or 6 oz)           | 8 cups       |
| Olive oil                                   | ½ tsp      | 1 tsp                           | 2 tsp        |
| Ground pork sausage, casings removed        | ½ lb       | 1 lb                            | 2 lb         |
| Dried rosemary or Italian seasoning, optional | ½ tsp    | 1 tsp                           | 2 tsp        |
| Chopped yellow onion                        | ⅜ cup      | ¾ cup (100g; ½ of a large onion) | 1½ cups      |
| Garlic cloves, minced                       | 1          | 2                               | 4            |
| Chopped bell peppers                        | 1 cup      | 2 cups (275g or 9–10 oz)        | 4 cups       |
| Sliced mushrooms                            | ½ cup      | 1 cup (100g or 3 oz)            | 2 cups       |
| Roughly chopped fresh spinach               | ½ cup      | 1 cup                           | 2 cups       |
| Large eggs                                  | 6          | 12                              | 24           |
| Salt                                        | ⅛ tsp      | ¼ tsp                           | ½ tsp        |
| Freshly ground black pepper                 | 1/16 tsp   | ⅛ tsp                           | ¼ tsp        |
| Milk or half-and-half                       | ⅓ cup      | ⅔ cup (160ml)                   | 1⅓ cups      |
| Shredded cheddar cheese                     | ½ cup      | 1 cup (100g or 3.5 oz)          | 2 cups       |
| Green onion and/or chopped parsley, optional garnish | to taste | to taste               | to taste     |

## Instructions

1. Grease a 9×13-inch dish, or any 3–4 quart oven-safe dish. Spread the bread cubes over the bottom in an even layer.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage, along with the rosemary or Italian seasoning if you're using it, breaking the sausage into bite-size pieces as it starts to cook. Add the onion, garlic, peppers, mushrooms and spinach, and cook until everything has softened slightly and the sausage is mostly cooked through, 5–6 minutes.
3. Take the sausage and vegetable mixture off the heat and spread it in an even layer over the bread.
4. Whisk the eggs, salt, pepper, milk and cheese together, then pour evenly over the sausage and vegetables. Add another sprinkle of salt and pepper on top.
5. Cover with plastic wrap or foil and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes and up to 24 hours. When you're ready to bake, let it sit at room temperature for 10–15 minutes while the oven heats.
6. Heat the oven to 375°F (191°C). Bake uncovered until the top is golden, the edges are crisp, and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, about 40–45 minutes.
7. Cool for 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
8. Leftovers keep in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Reheat in the microwave.

## Notes

- **Make ahead:** assemble through step 5 up to 24 hours before baking. The sausage and vegetable mixture can also be cooked up to 24 hours ahead, covered and refrigerated.
- **Freezing:** freeze unbaked through step 5, covered with plastic wrap then foil, for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator, then bring to room temperature before baking as directed. A baked casserole also freezes for up to 3 months — Sally suggests baking it in a disposable aluminum pan for this. Cool completely, cover with plastic wrap then foil, thaw about 24 hours covered in the refrigerator, remove the plastic, and bake at 350°F (177°C) for 25–30 minutes.
- **Bread:** crusty sourdough is excellent, or any crusty, hearty bread — the crustier or staler the better. Fresh or flimsy bread can be cubed and baked on a sheet at 300°F (149°C) for 10 minutes first; no need to cool it before layering.
- **Potatoes instead of bread:** use about 3 cups (around 380g) frozen diced hash browns, no need to thaw. Fresh potatoes work too, peeled, diced, and browned in a skillet with 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium heat for 10 minutes first.
- **Sausage or other meat:** pre-cooked sausage links or ham need no cooking — slice or chunk them and stir into the cooked vegetables. Ground turkey, beef or chicken can replace the sausage, pre-cooked with the vegetables. For bacon, reduce to ½ pound, cut into bite-size pieces, cook separately until browned, drain, then mix with the vegetables.
- **Other vegetables:** replace some or all of the peppers, mushrooms and spinach with chopped broccoli, sliced zucchini or squash, or kale, pre-cooked the same way.
- **Vegetarian:** leave out the meat and add another cup of vegetables.
- **Milk:** half-and-half or whole milk gives the richest texture; lower-fat or dairy-free milk works too.
- **Cheese:** Sally usually uses a blend of regular and sharp white cheddar. Any cheese you like works — pepper jack, feta, Swiss, smoked gouda.
