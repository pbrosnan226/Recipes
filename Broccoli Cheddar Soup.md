A creamy, filling soup with broccoli flavor built in layers.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Half batch | As written (6 servings) | Double batch |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------ |
| broccoli | ¾ pound | 1 1/2 pounds (700 g) broccoli | 3 pounds |
| vegetable oil | 1 tablespoon | 2 tablespoons (30 ml) vegetable oil | 4 tablespoons |
| kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper | as written | Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper | as written |
| unsalted butter | 1½ tablespoons | 3 tablespoons (45 g) unsalted butter | 6 tablespoons |
| onion | ½ | 1 medium onion, sliced (about 6 ounces; 170 g) | 2 |
| carrot | ½ | 1 medium carrot, peeled and finely diced (about 4 ounces; 120 g) | 2 |
| garlic | 1½ cloves | 3 medium cloves garlic, thinly sliced | 6 cloves |
| water or chicken stock | 1 cup | 2 cups (475 ml) water, or homemade or store-bought low-sodium chicken stock | 4 cups |
| whole milk | 1½ cups | 3 cups (700 ml) whole milk | 6 cups |
| russet potato | ½ | 1 small russet potato, peeled and sliced (about 4 ounces; 120 g) | 2 |
| sharp cheddar cheese | 6 ounces | 12 ounces (340 g) sharp cheddar cheese, grated (see notes) | 24 ounces |
| deli-style American cheese | 4 ounces | 8 ounces (240 g) deli-style American cheese, diced (see notes) | 16 ounces |
| mustard powder | ½ teaspoon | 1 teaspoon (3 g) mustard powder | 2 teaspoons |
| hot sauce | dash | Dash of hot sauce, such as Frank's RedHot | dash |

## Instructions

1. Split the broccoli into florets and stems. Cut the florets into bite-size pieces and set them aside. Chop the stems roughly and keep them separate.
2. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over high heat until it shimmers. Add the florets and leave them undisturbed until charred underneath, about 1 minute. Stir, season with salt and pepper, and keep cooking and stirring now and then until tender and charred in spots, about 1 minute more. Move to a rimmed baking sheet to cool.
3. Put the Dutch oven back over medium heat. Add the butter, onion, carrot, and broccoli stems, and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring often, until tender but not browned, about 5 minutes, lowering the heat if needed. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
4. Add the water or chicken stock, milk, and potato, and bring to a boil over high heat. Turn down to a bare simmer and cook, stirring now and then, until the broccoli and potato are completely tender, about 30 minutes.
5. Toss both cheeses with the mustard powder in a large bowl. Blend the soup until completely smooth with an immersion blender, or in batches in a countertop blender, adding the cheese a handful at a time. Stir in the hot sauce and adjust the salt and pepper. Stir in the reserved florets and pulse the blender a few more times, so a few pieces break down but most stay bite-size. Serve right away.

## Notes

- Deli-style American cheese can be found at the supermarket deli counter.
- Any mix of cheddar, American, and other young, moist cheeses, such as Jack or colby, works.
