Onion, garlic, ginger and spinach cooked down with fire roasted tomatoes, chickpeas, curry spices and coconut milk, served over basmati rice.

![Chickpea curry](https://www.acouplecooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Chickpea-Curry-007.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                                          | Half batch          | As written (4 servings)        | Double batch          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Yellow onion                                        | ½                   | 1                              | 2                     |
| Large garlic clove                                  | ½                   | 1                              | 2                     |
| Grated ginger                                       | ½ teaspoon          | 1 teaspoon                     | 2 teaspoons           |
| Coconut oil or olive oil                            | ½ tablespoon        | 1 tablespoon                   | 2 tablespoons         |
| Baby spinach leaves (or chopped spinach)            | 3 cups              | 6 cups (5 ounces)              | 12 cups               |

| Kosher salt                                         | ⅜ teaspoon          | ¾ teaspoon                     | 1½ teaspoons          |
| Chickpeas                                           | 1 (15-ounce) can    | 2 15-ounce cans                | 4 (15-ounce) cans     |

| White or brown basmati rice, to serve               | as needed           | as needed                      | as needed             |

## Instructions

1. If you're serving it with rice, start the basmati rice.
2. Mince the onion. Mince the garlic. Peel and mince the ginger, or grate it. Drain and rinse the chickpeas.
3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and sauté for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and spinach, and sauté for 2 minutes until the spinach is fully wilted.
4. Carefully pour in the tomatoes, then add the curry powder, cumin, coriander, salt and chickpeas. Cook for 5 minutes, until bubbly.
5. Stir in the coconut milk until fully combined, then take it off the heat. Serve with basmati rice. Leftovers keep up to 3 days refrigerated.

## Notes

- **Fire roasted tomatoes** are the key ingredient here: they're charred over an open fire before canning, so they taste sweet straight out of the can. Other varieties can taste bitter out of the can and need to cook longer — if you substitute high quality canned tomatoes, cook the sauce 10 minutes longer.
