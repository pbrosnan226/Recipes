Corn kernels charred hard in a hot skillet, then tossed off the heat with crumbled cheese, scallions, cilantro, jalapeño, mayonnaise, lime and chili powder.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet or wok over high heat until shimmering. Add the corn kernels, season to taste with salt, toss once or twice, then cook without moving until charred on one side, about 2 minutes. Toss and stir, and repeat until charred on the second side, about 2 minutes more. Keep tossing and charring until the corn is well charred all over, about 10 minutes in total. Transfer to a large bowl.
2. Add the cheese, scallions, cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, mayonnaise, lime juice and chili powder, and toss to combine. Taste and adjust with salt and more chili powder. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Special equipment: a large wok or large nonstick skillet, and a Microplane grater.
