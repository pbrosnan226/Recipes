Chicken breasts rubbed with dried onion and oregano, browned, then baked with softened onions, garlic, thyme and rosemary in beef stock.

![Oven-baked French onion chicken](https://paleoleap.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/french-onion-chicken-recipe.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                               | Half batch | As written (4 servings) | Double batch |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------ |
| Chicken breasts, skinless and boneless   | 1 to 2     | 2 to 4                  | 4 to 8       |
| Onions, sliced                           | 1 to 1½    | 2 to 3                  | 4 to 6       |
| Garlic cloves, minced                    | 1½ to 2    | 3 to 4                  | 6 to 8       |
| Fresh thyme, minced                      | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Fresh rosemary, minced                   | ½ tbsp     | 1 tbsp                  | 2 tbsp       |
| Beef stock                               | ¾ cup      | 1½ cups                 | 3 cups       |
| Dried onion                              | ½ tsp      | 1 tsp                   | 2 tsp        |
| Dried oregano                            | ½ tsp      | 1 tsp                   | 2 tsp        |
| Paprika, optional                        | ¼ tsp      | ½ tsp                   | 1 tsp        |
| Ghee                                     | ⅛ cup      | ¼ cup                   | ½ cup        |
| Cooking fat                              | as needed  | as needed               | as needed    |
| Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper | to taste   | to taste                | to taste     |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Combine the dried onion, dried oregano and paprika in a bowl, and season generously to taste.
3. Rub the chicken with the onion and oregano mixture, coating it well.
4. Melt cooking fat in a skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken 2 to 3 minutes per side, until nicely browned.
5. Set the chicken aside and add the ghee to the same skillet.
6. Add the fresh onion and garlic and cook over medium heat, stirring, until soft.
7. Add 2 tbsp of the stock to the skillet along with the thyme and rosemary, and cook until slightly caramelized, 5 to 6 minutes.
8. Return the chicken to the pan, toss everything together, pour in the remaining stock, and put the pan in the oven.
9. Bake until the chicken is cooked through, about 15 to 18 minutes.
10. Adjust the seasoning and serve.
