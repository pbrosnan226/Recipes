Shredded zucchini and onion squeezed dry, bound with egg, flour and Gruyère, and pan-fried with fresh corn kernels. Served with a herbed sour cream.

## Instructions

1. **Herb sour cream:** mix the sour cream (or yogurt), lemon zest, basil, chives and anchovy, if using, in a medium bowl. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside, or refrigerate until you're ready to serve.
2. **Fritters:** heat the oven to 250°F (120°C) and set a wire rack in a rimmed baking sheet. Grate the zucchini on the large holes of a box grater, or with the shredding blade of a food processor, and transfer it to a colander set over a bowl or in the sink. Grate the onion the same way and add it to the zucchini.
3. Toss the zucchini and onion with 1 teaspoon of the salt and let stand for 10 minutes. Squeeze out as much liquid as possible, using your hands or a clean dish towel. Transfer to a large bowl and add the corn kernels. Stir in the eggs, garlic, black pepper, flour, baking powder and cheese until thoroughly combined and no dry flour remains.
4. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large cast iron or heavy stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Working in batches so the pan isn't crowded, drop scant ⅓-cupfuls of batter into the skillet, then press down on each with the cup to flatten it to about ¼ inch thick.
5. Cook until the underside is golden brown, about 4 minutes. The corn tends to pop in the hot pan, so a splatter guard or lid is worth using. If the fritters darken too quickly, lower the heat to medium. Flip and cook until browned on the second side, 2 to 3 minutes more. Transfer to the prepared baking sheet and keep warm in the oven. Repeat with the remaining batter, keeping the pan well oiled as you go.
6. Serve the fritters warm, with a dollop of herb sour cream on top.

## Notes

- Special equipment: a large cast iron or heavy stainless steel skillet, a box grater or food processor with the shredding blade, a colander, a rimmed baking sheet, and a wire rack.
