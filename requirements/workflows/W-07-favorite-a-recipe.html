W-07 Favorite a recipe

Actors: Member, Supabase

Starts when: A Member opens a recipe page, or selects Favorites in the header.

Ends when: The favorite is saved or removed, or the Member sees their favorites.

flowchart LR
  subgraph sb["Supabase"]
    save["Save or remove the favorite"] --> okq{"Saved?"}
  end
  subgraph m["Member"]
    start(["Start"]) --> d{"What next?"}
    d -- "Select Favorite" --> s1["Step 1: Button shows Favorited"]
    d -- "Select Favorited" --> s2["Step 2: Favorite is removed"]
    d -- "Select Favorites in header" --> s3["Step 3: Home shows only my favorites"]
    s1 --> save
    s2 --> save
    okq -- "Yes" --> done(["End"])
    okq -- "No" --> err["Button returns to its old state, message says so"]
    err --> done
    s3 --> any{"Any favorites?"}
    any -- "Yes" --> done
    any -- "No" --> emp["Page says there are no favorites yet"]
    emp --> done
  end

Rounded: start and end. Box: a step. Click a step to open its screen. Diamond: a decision. Each band is one actor.
