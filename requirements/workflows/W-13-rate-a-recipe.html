W-13 Rate a recipe

Actors: Member, Supabase

Starts when: A signed-in Member opens a recipe page.

Ends when: The rating is saved or removed.

Needs a signed-in Member (W-06, W-14); no longer blocked, sign-in is planned.

flowchart LR
  subgraph sb["Supabase"]
    save["Save or remove the rating, clear Want to try on a rating"]
  end
  subgraph m["Member"]
    start(["Start"]) --> d{"What next?"}
    d -- "Tap a star (left half = half star)" --> s1["Step 1: Rating shown, Want to try cleared"]
    d -- "Tap another star" --> s2["Step 2: Rating changes"]
    d -- "Select Remove rating" --> s3["Step 3: Rating removed, recipe unmarked"]
    s1 --> save
    s2 --> save
    s3 --> save
    save --> ok{"Saved?"}
    ok -- "Yes" --> s4["Step 4: Average and count update"]
    ok -- "No" --> err["Row returns to its old state and says saving failed"]
    s4 --> done(["End"])
    err --> done
  end

Rounded: start and end. Box: a step. Click a step to open its screen. Diamond: a decision. Each band is one actor.
